# Commercial Licensing — AI English Test

Thank you for your interest in using AI English Test commercially.

## What counts as commercial?

- Using it in a cram school, company training, or paid course
- Running it as a hosted / SaaS service (even if the code is modified)
- Bundling it into a product you sell
- Internal business / client use

If in doubt, assume it is commercial and ask.

## How to get a license

1. Email **vitolai@gmail.com** with subject `[Commercial License] AI English Test`
2. Include:
   - Name / Company
   - Intended use (teaching / SaaS / resale / internal)
   - Estimated users or seats
   - Whether you need redistribution or modification rights
3. You will receive a quote + a signed commercial license file.
4. After payment, keep the license file with your deployment as proof.

## Typical tiers (adjust per quote)

- **Single classroom / small studio**: flat fee, 1-year updates
- **Company / chain**: per-site or per-seat, 1-year updates
- **SaaS / resale / OEM**: revenue share or annual, includes redistribution rights

## Non-commercial users

Students, self-learners, academic researchers: you are already covered
by OPTION 1 in `LICENSE` — free, no need to write in. Just keep the
copyright notice.

## Enforcement

Versions after 2026-10-10 are NOT MIT. Commercial use without a license
is copyright infringement. Old MIT copies (≤ 2026-10-10) stay MIT, but
receive no updates or support.
